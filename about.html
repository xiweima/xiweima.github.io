XIWEI
MA

Theatre Artist

Xiwei Ma is a theatre artist working across directing, devising, performance, facilitation and writing. Her practice is rooted in the body and in process — she works through improvisation, devising and workshop-based making, and tends to occupy the dual position of creator and performer at once.

She is interested in how gender, power and personal experience can be staged and transformed, and in what happens in the room before a work is finished. Her productions have been presented at the Wuzhen Theatre Festival, Shanghai Grand Theatre, Shanghai West Bund Theatre, Beijing Tianqiao Performing Arts Center, Rockbund Art Museum and Fotografiska Shanghai.

She trained at Shanghai Theatre Academy (Applied Theatre and Drama Education, 2020–2024), and has undertaken residencies at the Russian Institute of Theatre Arts (GITIS), Moscow (2024) and l’aria, the 8th International Meetings of Theatre Schools, France (2024). She has trained under Jacob Wye, Kirill Burlov and Samuli Nordberg (2022–2025), and holds a City Academy (London) teaching certification.
